Delete Directly in the Mobile App

If you are currently signed in on Android or iOS, you can delete your account immediately in under 30 seconds:

  1. Open the LocalPilot app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap the Profile / Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Account & Security → Connected Google Profiles.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm via password or biometric verification.
Note on Subscriptions: Deleting your LocalPilot account removes app data but does not automatically cancel active Apple App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Please cancel active renewals in your store subscription settings first.
