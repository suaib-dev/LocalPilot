1. Overview

Welcome to LocalPilot ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard personal and business information when you use the LocalPilot mobile application, web dashboard, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or connecting your Google Business Profile to LocalPilot, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service or request data removal on our Delete Account page.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Business Information

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, business email address, optional phone number, and authentication identifiers when you register or sign in.
  • Brand Voice Preferences: Tone selections, business category details, custom keywords, greeting templates, and auto-reply rules you configure inside the app.

2.2 Google Business Profile Data (With Your Explicit Consent)

  • Business location names, addresses, categories, opening/holiday hours, and service descriptions.
  • Customer reviews, star ratings, reviewer display names, and existing owner replies required to draft and publish responses.
  • Google Business posts, Q&A entries, photos you upload through LocalPilot, and performance insights (search views, map impressions, direction clicks, and call metrics).

2.3 Technical & Diagnostic Metadata

  • Device type, operating system version, app crash diagnostics, and security logs used solely to maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Limited Use Disclosure: LocalPilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, with respect to Google Business Profile API data:

  • We only request the minimum OAuth scopes necessary to read your business locations, read and reply to reviews, publish posts, and display performance analytics at your direction.
  • We do not use Google Workspace or Google Business Profile user data to train generalized foundation AI models.
  • We do not sell Google user data or allow human staff to read your private data unless required to resolve a support ticket with your explicit consent, for security investigations, or to comply with applicable law.
  • You may revoke LocalPilot's Google access anytime from your Google Account Security Settings or from our app settings.

4. AI Processing & Automation

When you enable AI review replies, post generation, or Q&A drafting, LocalPilot sends the relevant review text, business name, and selected brand tone instructions to our secure AI inference pipeline to generate contextual suggestions.

  • User Control: You choose whether AI drafts publish automatically (e.g., only for 4–5★ ratings) or wait in your approval queue.
  • No Cross-Customer Leakage: Your brand preferences and review drafts remain isolated to your workspace and are never exposed to other businesses.

5. Sharing & Trusted Subprocessors

We share information only in strictly limited circumstances:

  • Cloud Infrastructure & AI Inference Providers: Vetted infrastructure vendors bound by strict data processing agreements, confidentiality obligations, and zero-retention AI API policies where supported.
  • Payment Processors: If you subscribe to a paid plan, billing transactions are handled directly by Apple App Store, Google Play Billing, or PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways; LocalPilot never stores full credit card numbers.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by valid court order, subpoena, or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

6. Security & Data Retention

We protect your credentials and business data using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest for OAuth refresh tokens, role-based access controls, and continuous security monitoring.

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service. Upon account deletion, OAuth tokens are revoked immediately, cached Google Business data and AI drafts are permanently erased within 7 days, and encrypted backups expire within 30 days. Minimal billing/tax records may be kept only where mandated by applicable financial laws.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

Depending on your region, you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: Edit your profile, brand voice, or linked locations anytime inside the LocalPilot app.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Delete your account instantly inside the app or submit a verified deletion request via our Delete Account page.
  • Children's Privacy: LocalPilot is intended for business users aged 18 and older and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or 16 where applicable).

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, Google API data usage, or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact our Data Protection & Support Team:

  • Email: privacy@localpilot.ai
  • General Support: support@localpilot.ai
