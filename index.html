Everything you need to know before connecting your profile
Is GrahakHubAI safe to connect to my Google Business Profile?

Yes. GrahakHubAI uses official Google OAuth 2.0 authorization and strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. You grant scoped permissions only to the Business Profile locations you select, and you can revoke access anytime inside Google Account settings or in our app.

Can I review AI replies before they go live on Google?

Absolutely. You have full control: enable instant Autopilot for 4-star and 5-star reviews while holding 1–3 star reviews in an approval queue, or require manual one-tap approval for every reply and post.

Does GrahakHubAI support multiple branches or agency clients?

Yes! Whether you run a single neighborhood café or manage 500+ franchise locations, GrahakHubAI lets you group profiles, sync holiday hours in bulk, and compare location-level insights side by side.

How do I delete my GrahakHubAI account or disconnect my data?

You can permanently delete your account inside the GrahakHubAI mobile app under Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account, or submit an instant deletion request from our dedicated Delete Account page.
